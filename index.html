02 / My workflow

From a brief
to a useful handoff.

  1. 01

    Define the target

    Agree geography, company type, exclusions and what makes an account relevant.

  2. 02

    Research & document

    Read public sources, capture evidence and distinguish observed facts from assumptions.

  3. 03

    Clean & prioritize

    Deduplicate domains, identify contact routes and flag missing buyer information.

  4. 04

    Outreach & handoff

    Prepare relevant messages; after campaign approval, qualify replies and hand over meetings with context.
